Striking Visuals

It seems statistics on the internet all have the same feeling to them. Polished? Yes. As good as they can be? No. Except for the occasional chart that is made to be beautiful by professional graphic designers and experienced hobbyists, there aren't many out there. At Noric Dausen, we believe in a new future. One where stunning charts don't require professionals to make them. One where anyone can make statistics beautiful. To do this, we are developing statstical technology that can open the door to making graphs that are clear and beautiful.

Ease of Use

One of the largest issues with modern statistical visualization technology is how difficult the programs are to use. Many look and feel like ancient relics from a bygone era of computer technology. This makes creating statstics with them feel clunkly, hard to understand, and generally a poor experience. Noric Dausen is working to make statistical visualization feel modern, intuitive, and easy to use. Clear and well explained tutorials will make learning statistical visualization programs easy while modular design will make the entire process feel like it builds off of what's already known.

Extensive Capability

Visualizing Statistics often is made difficult simply by the fact that you are highly limited in your options. Often times you may have to settle for a chart of graph that technically "shows the data" but in a less than satisfactory way. Noric Dausen understands that and we are going to change that. Breaking the chains of what kinds charts are available and expanding what is possible. Our statistical visualization technology will have extensive features that work in tandem to make your imagination the only limit on possibilities.

Interactability

Data is static, it is unchanging, but the way we view it doesn't have to be. Spreadsheets are surely the most difficult way to understand data. A spreadsheet easily bores many who see it. To help better communicate data, spreedsheets are converted to graphs, charts, and maps. Still, these can be overwhelming, or not specific enough on precise details. The most engaging way to solve this is with interactable statistics. Maps with levels of detail, graphs with selectable series, and charts with expandable sections. Noric Dausen understands this and develops technology that makes creating statistics with high levels of interactablilty more than just a dream.
